For lawyers and partners

Will My Crypto is a planning tool for people who hold crypto in self-custody. It never holds coins, keys, or seed phrases, and it does not give legal advice. Families still need attorneys who understand digital assets, so we are building a network of estate planning, trusts, tax, and technology professionals.

What we're looking for

  • Estate planning and probate attorneys comfortable with digital assets, in any state or country
  • Trust, tax and elder-law practitioners
  • Security reviewers, auditors, and cryptographers
  • Notaries, fiduciaries, and other trusted-person services

No commitment is created by this form. We'll reply by email to talk about fit, jurisdiction coverage, and how reviews would work.

Do not include seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or client information in this form. We will never ask for them.

We use this only to reply to you. See the privacy page.

Will My Crypto provides planning tools and information only. It is not a law firm, financial advisor, custodian, or wallet provider, and does not give legal, tax, or financial advice. No tool can guarantee that digital assets will be recoverable. Laws vary by location; consult a qualified attorney and tax professional. Never share your seed phrase or private keys with anyone, including us.
